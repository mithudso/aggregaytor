Preference Learning

The ML model learns your type from how you interact with profiles. It uses logistic regression trained on body type, position, age, ethnicity, and other features.
How to train the model ▸
  1. Open Grindr in a tab (for block/hidden list import)
  2. Click Import Grindr Lists — imports all blocked/hidden profiles as negative signals
  3. Click Train Now — scans all contacts for pins, favorites, blocks, ratings, and bookmarks
  4. Use 👍/👎 buttons on profiles and ⭐ star ratings for ongoing training
  5. Click Full Retrain to rebuild the model from scratch with all data
Positive signals: Sniffies pins 📌, Grindr favorites ⭐, bookmarks, ratings ≥4
Negative signals: blocks, hides, ratings ≤2
Features learned: body type, position, age, ethnicity, height, photo presence, profile text keywords

Grindr Auto-Login

Grindr rate-limits block actions and will force a re-login after too many. Save your email + password here and the extension will fill the login form for you the moment it detects you've been logged out — so the block queue can resume without you noticing. Credentials are encrypted (AES-GCM with a device-local key) and never leave your machine.

⚠ Only works with email + password login. If you use "Sign in with Apple" / Google / Facebook, this won't help — those go through OAuth popups that the extension can't drive. Switch Grindr to a plain email + password account if you want auto-login to work.
